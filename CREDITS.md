# Original creators, rights, and free access

## A personal fan project

Mood Swings Online is maintained by [Ruben / JollyRogerz](https://github.com/JollyRogerz) to enjoy a favorite physical game with friends online. Ruben owns two physical Mood Swings decks, supports the original makers, and is excited for future releases.

**The game and all gameplay features remain free.** No paid access, subscriptions, advertisements, in-game purchases, or paid card unlocks are offered. The maintainer pays for hosting and accepts optional USDC donations without rewards. These donations go to JollyRogerz, not the original creators or Wizards of the Coast. Their inclusion does not establish authorization under Wizards’ Fan Content Policy or any separate license.

## Original work and attribution

Mood Swings and its original card text, rules presentation, artwork, visual identity, names, and branding remain the work of Wizards of the Coast and their respective creators and rights holders. Hasbro, Wizards of the Coast, and Secret Lair names and marks belong to their owners. Individual illustrations retain the artist credits shown on the original cards.

This is an unofficial fan adaptation. No affiliation, sponsorship, approval, or endorsement is claimed. Public availability of source material, ownership of physical decks, attribution, and a noncommercial intention do not establish a license to redistribute someone else's work. This repository does not grant any rights to the original game or assets. No blanket open-source license covering those materials is offered. Dependency licenses remain applicable to their respective packages.

If a rights holder has a concern, please contact the maintainer through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/JollyRogerz) or [repository issues](https://github.com/JollyRogerz/mood-swings/issues) so it can be addressed, including removing affected material where appropriate.

## Thank you

The original game's [published history](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/the-history-of-mood-swings) identifies Mark Rosewater as its designer and credits Corey Bowen's play-design work, Michael Zhang's editing, and Colby Nichols's visual design. It also thanks Matt Danner, Ari Zirulnik, Jefferson Dunlap, Megan Kenreck, Silvia Cortese, Lisa Hanson, Christina Troup, Zakeel Gordon, Lucas Harrington, Lynne Rosewater, Lora Rosewater, and Mark Heggen for their contributions and support. Please read the original article for the fuller account; this acknowledgment is not a replacement for the official credits.

Thank you as well to every artist whose work appears on the cards and to the wider teams who made the physical game possible. Colby Nichols describes the art direction, frames, card back, and packaging in [Crafting the Visual Identity of Mood Swings](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/crafting-the-visual-identity-of-mood-swings).

## Fruit fly connectome data

The Fly brain bot uses wiring from **MaleCNS v1.0**, the complete adult male *Drosophila* central nervous system connectome produced by HHMI Janelia Research Campus, Google Research, and collaborators, released in September 2026 under the CC-BY 4.0 license. The extracted circuit in `data/fly/circuit.json` records the source files and checksums. Thank you to the FlyEM and FlyWire teams and every proofreader behind that decade of work. See [docs/fly-brain.md](docs/fly-brain.md).
