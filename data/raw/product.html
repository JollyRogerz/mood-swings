About mood swings

Mood Swings is a light strategy trading card game for two to four people. Each game comes with forty-five randomized cards from a possible one hundred thirty-three. The game is playable out of the box and takes five to ten minutes to play.

Read more

How to play Mood Swings


Step 1
Each round, every player plays a card. You then add up the scores of all the cards in play and the highest scorer wins the round. Win three rounds and win the game.

Step 2
The winner goes first during the next round and the losing player(s) draws a card. Once cards are played, they stay in play unless another card removes it.

Step 3
Cards can have effects when you play them and some cards even alter the rules, so anything’s possible!

FAQ

Mood Swings is a light, fast strategy card came that will take from five to ten minutes to play.

Each game comes with forty-five game cards, a rules card, and a play aide card. It has everything you need to play with two to four people right out of the box.

Mood Swings is what's known as a trading card game, meaning the forty-five cards you open up in your copy of the game won’t be the exact same forty-five cards that everyone else opens up. The game is playable right out of the box, and all the cards represent moods and emotions.
